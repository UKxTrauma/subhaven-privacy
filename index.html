Privacy Policy for SubHaven

Developer: UKxTrauma • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

SubHaven is a 100% offline utility. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, operates no backend servers, and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or financial data.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All subscription entries, free-trial dates, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device. If you export a PDF, CSV, or JSON backup, that file is created locally and shared only where you explicitly choose.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional lifetime Pro upgrades are processed directly by Google Play Billing on your device. SubHaven never receives your payment details.

4. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, contact UKxTrauma via the developer contact email listed on the SubHaven Google Play Store page.
